Qualitative comparison of available denoising checkpoints on four fixed benchmark examples. Columns show the noisy input, DnCNN [1], Restormer [3], MaskDenoising [10], B0, Ours (G1), and ground truth. Every method receives the same original floating-point noisy image within a row. All displayed panels use the same recorded 96 × 96 center crop, with nearest-neighbor magnification and no brightness, contrast, gamma, or local image adjustment. Noisy panels are clipped to the display range only; this does not clip model inputs. Labels report PSNR (dB)/SSIM for each whole evaluated image, not for the displayed crop. B0 and G1 use their 300k-step training seed 0 checkpoints; these are individual examples, not three-seed means. The external methods use released pretrained checkpoints, whose training regimes may differ from the exact checkpoints underlying Table I. This is a newly evaluated visual supplement, not a reconstruction of the published table scores. The Speckle row retains the earlier Gaussian multiplicative-noise extension (variance 0.03), which differs from MATLAB's uniform multiplicative-noise protocol. MaskDenoising retains its published stochastic attention masking at inference with the per-image mask seed recorded in the inference metrics. HAT [11] and DIL [12] are omitted because matching weights were unavailable; no substitute or fabricated result panels are included. The rows were inherited from the existing report rather than selected according to the new methods' scores. No ranking, significance test, error bars, or cross-seed uncertainty is inferred from these individual images.
